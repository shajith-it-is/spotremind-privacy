Privacy Policy — SpotRemind

Last updated: November 25, 2026

SpotRemind ("the app") is developed by AlifBay as part of the AlifBay KhairHub ecosystem. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What the app does

SpotRemind lets you set reminders tied to physical places. When your device enters or leaves an area you defined, the app shows you a local notification with your reminder message.

Location data

To make location-based reminders work, the app accesses your device's location, including in the background while the app is closed, so it can detect when you arrive at, stay in, or leave a saved place.

  • Your location is processed on your device only.
  • Your location is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • We do not store a history of where you have been.
  • Location is used only to compare your current position against the reminders you created.

Data storage

All of your data — your reminders, coordinates, messages, schedules, checklists, and settings — is stored locally on your device using your operating system's standard local storage. We do not operate a backend server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app or deleting reminders in the app removes this data.

Maps

The app uses Google Maps to let you pick and view locations. Your use of the map is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Platform terms.

Permissions we request

  • Location (including background / "Allow all the time"): required to detect arrival at or departure from your saved places while the app is closed.
  • Notifications: required to deliver your location reminders and quick actions.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Doing so will stop location reminders from working.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
